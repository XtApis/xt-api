# Coin Research and Token Security

Powered by the `xt-coin-research` skill. No API Key required.

---

## Fundamentals

`market_coin_profile(coin)` — `coin` can be `btc` or `bitcoin`. It returns:

- Market cap rank, current market cap, **FDV (fully diluted valuation)**
- Circulating supply / total supply
- All-time high (ATH) and the drawdown since
- Sector classification and project profile

```
You: How do ARB's fundamentals look
AI:  [ARB / Arbitrum] Market cap rank #42, market cap 1.24B USD
     FDV 3.87B USD — FDV/market cap ≈ 3.1, a large share of tokens is still locked
     Circulating 3.2B / total 10B (32%)
     ATH $2.40 (2023-01), -87% since
     Sector: Layer 2, Ethereum ecosystem
```

**The FDV / market cap ratio** is the key to judging unlock pressure: the higher the ratio, the more tokens are yet to circulate and the greater the future sell pressure.

## Comparing Multiple Coins

```
You: Compare ARB, OP, and MATIC
```

The assistant fetches all three profiles in parallel and lays them out side by side by market cap, FDV, circulation rate, and sector, then combines that with [market data](./MarketData) to describe relative performance.

## On-Chain Token Security Check

`market_token_security(chain, contract_address)` — a must before buying any small-cap token.

Checks performed:

| Item | Risk it indicates |
|------|-------------------|
| **Honeypot** | You can buy but not sell; funds are locked outright |
| **Buy/sell tax** | High tax rates eat into principal; 10%+ warrants serious caution |
| **Open source** | A closed-source contract cannot be audited for behavior |
| **Mint authority** | The team can mint without limit and dilute holders |
| **Holder concentration** | If top addresses hold too much, a single address can dump the price |

```
You: Is this contract safe — 0xabc...def (BSC)

AI: [Token security check · BSC]
    ✅ Not a honeypot
    ⚠️ Buy tax 5% / sell tax 12% — sell tax is clearly high
    ✅ Contract is open source
    ❌ Mint authority exists, owner has not renounced
    ⚠️ Top 10 addresses hold 68%, high concentration
    [Conclusion] High risk. Un-renounced mint authority + high sell tax + concentrated holdings;
                 large positions are not advisable.
```

> The security check is an automated scan of public on-chain data. It **catches common traps but cannot guarantee the absence of risk**. Passing the check does not mean the team will not rug.
