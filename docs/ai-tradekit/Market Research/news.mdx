# News & Announcements

Powered by the `xt-news` skill. No API Key required.

---

## Tools

| Tool | Purpose | Parameters |
|------|---------|------------|
| `xt_announcements` | XT.COM official announcements: listings, delistings, deposit/withdrawal suspension and resumption, maintenance, campaigns | `locale`, `limit` |
| `xt_announcement_search` | Search official announcements by keyword | `keyword`, `locale`, `limit` |
| `market_news` | Aggregated crypto news feed in reverse chronological order, filterable by keyword/coin | `keyword`, `lang` (`en`/`zh`), `limit` |

`locale` supports 10 languages: `en-us` / `zh-cn` / `zh-tw` / `ko-kr` / `ru` / `es` / `tr` / `vi` / `ar` / `fa`.

---

## Intent Routing

| What you ask | What the assistant uses |
|--------------|-------------------------|
| "Any recent news?" | `market_news` |
| "What has XT listed recently?" | `xt_announcements` |
| "Is XX listed on XT?" | `xt_announcement_search(keyword=XX)` |
| "Why is XX up/down?" | `market_news(keyword=XX)` + `xt_announcement_search` + market/derivatives metrics |
| "When is XT's maintenance?" | `xt_announcement_search(keyword=maintenance)` |

## Attributing Price Moves

The most valuable use — lining up the news flow with the market setup:

```
You: Why is SOL up so much today

AI: [Price]   sol_usdt 24h +9.4%, turnover +180% vs yesterday
    [News]
      · 06:20 An ETF issuer filed an amendment for a spot SOL ETF (aggregated feed)
      · 09:05 TVL of a SOL ecosystem protocol grew 40% in a day (aggregated feed)
    [XT announcements] No related listing or maintenance announcement
    [Market]  OI +14% over 24h, funding rate rose from 0.008% to 0.031%,
              24M USD of shorts liquidated in the last 12h
    [Attribution] A sentiment-driven rally triggered by the ETF news, amplified by
                  concentrated short covering. Funding is already clearly elevated,
                  so chasing longs is getting expensive.
```

## Data Source Notes

- XT announcements are **official data**
- The news feed comes from **TradeKit aggregated public sources**; outlet names may be retained in the output, and no other exchange is involved

The feed is delayed and may contain unverified information — cross-check it yourself.
